About ChinaChallengeCoin

Fifteen years of turning ideas into coins people carry, trade, and keep.

Our Story

ChinaChallengeCoin began as a small metal-craft workshop with a handful of craftsmen and a simple belief: a challenge coin should feel as meaningful as the moment it marks. What started with engraving tools and a few enamel ovens has grown into a full factory serving customers in more than fifty countries.

Over fifteen years we've struck, filled, and polished millions of coins — for military units, police and fire departments, corporate teams, clubs, and commemorative events. No matter the size of the order, every coin gets the same care at every step.

What keeps us going is the same thing that started it: the look on someone's face when a design they imagined is finally solid metal in their hand. We're here to make that happen, one coin at a time, with no minimum order getting in the way.
